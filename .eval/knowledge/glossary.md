# Glossary

_Terms this company uses in a specific way, as their own material defines them._

## DJTILBUD

The two-sided marketplace for customers seeking musicians for events and musicians (DJs or saxophonists) who make quotes on these requests.

> "Mobile app for DJTILBUD which is a 2 sided marketplace for customers seeking musicians for events and musicians that makes quotes on these requests, offering a service as either DJ og Saxopohnists."
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`


## profile-coach

An AI in the product that helps musicians figure out what is missing from their profile and what they should add to finalize a good profile.

> "One that helps the musicians write a quote (it gives a draft for the sales pitch) and another called "profile-coach" that helps them figuring out what is missing and what they should add to finalize a good profile"
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`


## Quote
