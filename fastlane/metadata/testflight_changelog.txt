New: Notification Center 🔔

All your notifications are now collected in one place, so you never miss a new job, a message, or an accepted offer.

• Tap the bell at the top of your Profile to see every notification you've received
• Tap a notification to jump straight to the job, chat, or offer it's about
• The unread count shows on the app icon, the Profile tab, and the bell
• Swipe to mark as read — or mark all read in one tap

Plus minor bug fixes and improvements.
