Venue tab on partner ext jobs

• The ext job detail is now tabbed for recurring-customer (partner) bookings: "Job" keeps the process tracker, customer contact, extra hours, chat, song requests, invoice status and the job info card; "Stedet" holds the venue badge, the event address, the new venue photos and the "Til festen" card
• Venue photos come from the admin tool (recurring customer -> Billeder tab), each with a comment. They are served by GET /api/internal-dj/ext-jobs as venue_photos per job, same endpoint as the "Fast kunde" name
• A partner booking with no photos yet shows an empty state on the tab; a non-partner ext job has no tab bar and looks exactly as before

What to test:
1. PARTNER JOB: open an ext job assigned to you that belongs to a recurring customer (e.g. Kokkedal). The app bar must show two tabs, "Job" and "Stedet". "Job" must contain everything it did before except the address and the "Til festen" card.
2. PHOTOS: have an admin upload two photos with comments on that account (admin -> Faste kunder -> account -> Billeder). Pull to refresh or reopen the job. "Stedet" must show a horizontal strip with the comment under each thumbnail. Tap one: full-screen viewer, swipe to the next, pinch to zoom, comment under the image, "1 / 2" in the app bar.
3. EMPTY STATE: on a partner account with no photos and a booking without wishes, "Stedet" must show "Ingen oplysninger om stedet endnu", not a blank screen.
4. NORMAL EXT JOB: open a non-partner ext job. No tab bar, address and notes inline as before.
5. CHAT BUBBLE: the floating "Beskeder" bubble must be visible on both tabs.
